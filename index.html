About Me

I am a first-year PhD student in the Graduate Institute of Science Education at National Taiwan Normal University (NTNU). My research centers on applying machine learning and digital technologies to investigate cognitive processes in digital learning.

My work focuses on integrating hardware and software — specifically eye-tracking and digital handwriting devices — to create Complete Problem Solving (CPS) environments. I develop programs that trace learners' dynamic problem-solving trajectories to accurately diagnose cognitive difficulties as they arise, enabling timely teacher interventions and adaptive learning systems.

With a background in Applied Mathematics and Optimization, I bridge rigorous quantitative methods with educational research to better understand how students think, struggle, and learn.
